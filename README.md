**The Gilded Vault** — a top-down midnight heist in the Marlow Gallery. Play Wren, a thief in a hooded cloak, sneaking past three patrolling guards with vision cones and two sweeping security cameras to steal the Star Sapphire, the Golden Idol, the Jade Mask and the Moon Pearl — then slip out the door below before the alarm meter hits 100%.

- One gallery room, all of it visible in a single frame: guards and their cones, cameras, loot and the alarm meter
- Detection with a chance to recover: suspicion rises in sight, falls in shadow; sneaking (Shift) halves how fast you are spotted
- Toss a coin (click or Space) to pull a guard off your trail and investigate the clink
- Synthesised WebAudio sounds: pickup chimes, coin clinks, alarm stings — no audio files

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
